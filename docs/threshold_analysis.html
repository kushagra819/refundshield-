Why it matters. An unusual concentration immediately below a decision threshold can indicate that behaviour is adapting to the decision boundary rather than occurring naturally. RefundShield does not flag accounts merely because they are close to 20%: it looks for excess concentration of reliable account return rates immediately below the threshold relative to the surrounding population. This threshold-evasion signal is later combined with cross-account relationships and behavioural similarity to identify coordinated risk.

Important. Threshold-evasion analysis identifies a population-level pattern. It does not determine that any individual customer is fraudulent. Prototype scoring logic — synthetic demonstration, not a validated fraud probability.
